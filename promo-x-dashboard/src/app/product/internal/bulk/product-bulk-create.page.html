Format

  • A header row with externalId, sku and name, and optionally categoryIds, in any order.
  • Each field up to 255 characters. Quote a field that contains commas.
  • Category IDs are promo-x's IDs, separated by ;.
  • External IDs must be new and unique within the storefront.
